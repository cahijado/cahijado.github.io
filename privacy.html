Retro 80s Car Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026 · Versión en español

Retro 80s Car Launcher is an Android home screen. It has no servers of its own, no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and the developer receives no data whatsoever from people who use it.

Everything the app stores stays in the app's private storage on the phone. The only thing that leaves the device is described under "Voice conversation", and it goes to Google, not to us.

What the app uses, and what for

Location

The dashboard speedometer needs precise location to compute speed. The position is used in memory while the screen is open: it is not stored, not logged and not sent anywhere. If the permission is denied, the app works the same and the speedometer reads zero.

Microphone and voice conversation

The microphone is opened only for the duration of a conversation, after the microphone button is pressed. There is no background listening and no wake word.

  • Audio is transcribed by Android's own speech recognizer. Depending on the phone and the installed language pack, that transcription happens on the device or on Google's servers, under the operating system's own terms. The app neither records nor stores the audio.
  • The text of the transcribed question, the last few conversation turns (kept in memory only, lost when the app closes) and the configured driver name are sent to the Google Gemini API to produce the answer.
  • That request is made with the API key each user creates and configures inside the app. The relationship is between the user and Google: Google's privacy policy and the Gemini API terms apply. The developer of the app is not involved, cannot see those conversations and receives no copy of anything.
  • The answer is spoken through the system's text-to-speech engine and is not stored.

With no Gemini key configured, the conversation feature is disabled: the microphone button shows as locked and the microphone is never opened.

Gemini API key

The key is stored in the app's private storage on the phone. It is excluded from cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it never leaves the phone it was typed into. It is sent only to Google, with each request, as the API's own credential. It can be removed at any time by clearing the field on the settings screen.

Notification access

Notification access is optional and granted by hand in Android's settings. The app uses it for two things, both on the device:

  • controlling music playback (knowing what is playing, pausing, skipping), which is the only route Android gives a normal app for this;
  • showing the turn-by-turn instruction from whichever navigation app is currently guiding.

Notification content is not stored, not written to any file and not sent to any server. Without this permission the app works the same, with those two panels empty.

List of installed apps

Like any home screen, the app queries which apps have a launcher icon so it can show and open them. That query is answered on the device and is not transmitted.

Settings

The driver name, the pursuit mode setting and the chosen indicators are stored on the phone. Like the key, they are excluded from backup and from transfer to another device.

What the app does NOT do

  • It sends no data to the developer.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or advertising IDs.
  • It creates no accounts and asks for no sign-up.
  • It does not record audio or store it on the device.
  • It keeps no conversation history beyond the open session.
  • It does not sell or share personal data with third parties.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app does with data, this page will be updated and the date in the header will change.

Reporting an answer

If an answer from the assistant is offensive or inappropriate, the settings screen has a button that opens your mail app with that question and answer already written, so you can report it. You send the message yourself: the app never sends anything without you seeing it.
